## Where things live

- `brand/style-guide.pdf`: the look, at a glance.
- `brand/brand.css`: colors, fonts, box style and safe area. Blocks use only
  these variables.
- `brand/feedback.md`: every Keep and Don't. A Don't never comes back.
- `brand/references.md`: the references behind the look.
- `blocks/`: the blocks they've kept, each with a preview.
- `sounds/catalog.md`: every sound, its role and its license.
- `videos/<date-name>/`: one folder per video (raw, storyboard, revisions,
  exports, retro, project).
