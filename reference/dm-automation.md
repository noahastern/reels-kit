# DM automation

A comment-to-DM automation ("comment EDIT and I'll send you the link") turns a
video's viewers into followers and leads. The kit offers to set one up at two
moments, and only when it's real.

## When to offer

- **End of `/onboard`:** once. "You're ready to go. Want to set up a DM
  automation for your videos?" If they say no, note it in their `CLAUDE.md`
  and never ask again unless they bring it up.
- **End of `/signoff`:** only when this video offers something people could
  get in a DM: a guide, template, recipe, link, product, discount or freebie,
  or the script already says "comment…". Then: "This would be a great video
  for a DM automation: people comment [word] and get [thing]." If the video
  doesn't fit, say nothing.
- Never more than one offer per video.

## How

The kit uses [DM Spark](https://dmspark.io) for Instagram.

- **If the DM Spark connector is connected** (its tools are available), read
  its guide first, then draft the automation: the keyword, a follow gate if
  they want only followers to get it, the message and the link. Leave it
  paused and give them the link to review it and turn it on. Never turn one
  on yourself unless they explicitly ask.
- **If it isn't connected:** one line on what DM Spark does and where to get
  it, then move on.
