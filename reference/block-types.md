# The six block types

The kit ships the types, not designs. `/onboard` builds each creator's first
version of every type from scratch in their brand, and their `blocks/` grows
from there. The types also give them words to direct with: "a proof card
here", "the point box again".

Every block is a HyperFrames sub-composition that uses only `brand.css`
variables, sizes to its content, and stays inside the safe area.

| # | Type | What it's for | Where | Timing | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Captions** | The words as they're said | Lower third, wherever no graphic holds it | Up to 3 words at a time, each landing as it's said; about 6 emphasis words per video in the accent | none |
| 2 | **Hook** | Text on frame 0 that says why to stay | Top of the safe area, or behind the head | On from frame 0, out on a jump cut | pop or whoosh |
| 3 | **Point box** | The main idea of a beat | On the chest, around (500, 1250) | In 0.5s before the first word, typed on the spoken words, out about 1s after the payoff | paper slide in, typing under |
| 4 | **List / steps** | A list where each item lands as it's said | Chest or beside the head | Each item lands inside its sentence | pop per item |
| 5 | **Proof** | A screenshot, stat, before/after, a real number | Where they point, or beside the head | Lands on the claim it proves | pop, ping on the number |
| 6 | **Emphasis screen** | A full-frame rule or closing callout | Full frame | Cuts in on a jump cut, out about 1s after the payoff | whoosh in, ping on the key word |

Zooms (static punch-ins and zoom ramps) are motion defaults, not blocks
(`reference/craft.md`).
